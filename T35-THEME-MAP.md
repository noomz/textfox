# T35 theme color map (V22, issue #1)

Generated by `tools/firefox-chrome/theme-color-map.py` from the sha256-pinned XPIs in
`ff_themes.py` (11 Nova themes × light/dark, Rosy Sky 4.0, Purple starfield 1.0, Topo! Black 1.0).
Firefox fallbacks for unset keys come from Firefox 160.0 `LightweightThemeManager.sys.mjs`,
`LightweightThemeConsumer.sys.mjs`, `ThemeVariableMap.sys.mjs` and `design-system/toolbar.css`.
These numbers are a model: contrast as Firefox would paint each pair with no art. The painted
checks in step 5 decide.

Fallback per V22: a pair the theme leaves unset, or one under 4.5:1, paints `--tf-bg`
(= `--lwt-accent-color` = `frame`) with `contrast-color()` text.

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-18)

- Accent: `tab_line` where it reaches 3:1 against `frame`, else today's `--tf-accent`
  (`--toolbarbutton-icon-fill`). Nova keeps its current accent; Purple starfield and Topo! Black
  take their line color.
- Focused URL bar: not mapped. The field keeps its `toolbar_field` pair when focused; the
  `--tf-accent` ring shows focus (V12).

## For review

1. **Accent (`tab_line`) fails 3:1 against `frame` on 21 of 22 Nova variants.** Nova lines are
   pale on light frames and dark on dark frames (e.g. ash light (183,182,186) on (239,237,242)).
   The table uses WCAG non-text contrast (3:1). V12 already requires focus rings at 3:1, and
   `--tf-accent` paints hover borders and focus rings. Fallback keeps today's `--tf-accent`
   (`--toolbarbutton-icon-fill`).
2. **Nova sets no `popup`**, so popups fall back on all 22 Nova variants (today's look).
3. **Focused URL bar (`toolbar_field_focus`, `toolbar_field_text_focus`)** is not in V22's list.
   Nova dark variants set it to (39,36,41), not their field color, so the field changes color
   on focus. Proposal: leave it out of the mapping (the field keeps its unfocused pair).
4. **Translucent backgrounds.** Nova `toolbar` is white 40% (light) or black 20% (dark), and
   Purple starfield's field, selected tab and popup are translucent. With art (V19) these lie
   over the art, not over `frame`. Only painted checks with art can judge them.
5. **B27 (Rosy Sky URL bar).** The URL bar keeps the theme's own field (225,184,182) with black
   text, 11.7:1. That is the theme's look the user asked for.
6. Rosy Sky's window and navbar text (225,184,182) on (204,112,133) is 1.9:1, so both fall back
   to the frame color with black text (6.2:1). Purple starfield's tab strip text is 4.40:1, just
   under the floor, so it falls back to white text.
